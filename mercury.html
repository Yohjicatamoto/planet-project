Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.

Mercury is a small, grey, heavily cratered world that looks a lot like our Moon. It races around the Sun faster than any other planet.

It has almost no atmosphere to trap heat, so its days are scorching and its nights are freezing.

Mercury spins slowly while it moves quickly around the Sun. Because of this, one full day from sunrise to sunrise lasts 176 Earth days, two of Mercury's years.
Mercury's metal core takes up about 85% of the planet's radius. That makes it the second densest planet after Earth.
Even this close to the Sun, there is water ice. It hides in deep craters near the poles that never get any sunlight.
Only a few missions have gone to Mercury: Mariner 10 in 1974-75, MESSENGER, which orbited from 2011 to 2015, and the European-Japanese BepiColombo.
