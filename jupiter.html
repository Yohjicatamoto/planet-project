Jupiter

The biggest planet in the solar system.

Jupiter is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. It has no solid surface, just clouds that get thicker and hotter the deeper you go.

Its colored stripes are bands of clouds blown by powerful winds, and its Great Red Spot is a storm that has lasted for hundreds of years.
