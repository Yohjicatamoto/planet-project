Solar System Explorer

the Sun, the Moon and seven planets.

Our solar system formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a spinning cloud of gas and dust. At its center is the Sun, a star that holds everything else in orbit with its gravity.

Eight planets travel around the Sun. The four inner planets Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are small and rocky. The four outer planets Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune are giants made mostly of gas and ice. Along with them are dwarf planets like Pluto, hundreds of moons, and many asteroids and comets.
