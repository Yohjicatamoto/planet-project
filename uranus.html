Uranus

The ice giant that spins on its side.

Uranus is an ice giant. Under its hazy blue-green clouds is a thick layer of water, ammonia and methane ices around a small rocky core.

It is tipped over so far that it basically rolls around the Sun on its side, which gives it extreme seasons that last about 21 years each.
