Earth

The third planet from the Sun.

Earth is the only place we know of where life exists. It sits at just the right distance from the Sun for liquid water to stay on its surface, and its atmosphere keeps the temperature steady and blocks harmful radiation.

Seen from space, it is a blue planet with white swirls of cloud, brown and green continents, and white ice at the poles.
