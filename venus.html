Venus

The hottest planet, wrapped in thick yellow clouds.

Venus is almost the same size as Earth, but it is a very different place. Its air is thick carbon dioxide, and its clouds are made of sulfuric acid.

That heavy atmosphere traps heat like a blanket, so Venus is even hotter than Mercury, though it is twice as far from the Sun.
