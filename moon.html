The Moon

Earth's only natural satellite.

The Moon is a dry, rocky world covered in craters and fine grey dust. Most scientists think it formed when a Mars-sized object crashed into the young Earth and threw rock into orbit.

Its gravity pulls on Earth's oceans and causes the tides. It is also the only other world people have walked on.

Facts
Property Value
Diameter 3,475 km about a quarter of Earth's
Distance from Earth About 384,400 km on average
Time to orbit Earth 27.3 days
Cycle of phases 29.5 days from new Moon to new Moon
Gravity About 1/6 of Earth's (16.5%)
Temperature About 127 C in sunlight to −173 °C at night
People who walked on it 12 astronauts, between 1969 and 1972
Rotation Tidally locked the same side always faces Earth
