Saturn

The planet with the brightest rings.

Saturn is the second largest planet. Like Jupiter, it is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium, with pale gold cloud bands.

Its famous rings are made of billions of pieces of ice and rock, from tiny grains to chunks as big as a house.

Saturn properties
The main rings stretch about 280,000 km across, but in most places they are only about 10 meters thick. They are mostly water ice, which is why they shine so brightly.
Titan is Saturn's largest moon and is bigger than the planet Mercury. It is the only moon with a thick atmosphere, and it has lakes of liquid methane and ethane.
At Saturn's north pole, a jet stream forms a giant six-sided shape. Each side of the hexagon is wider than Earth.
The Cassini spacecraft orbited Saturn from 2004 to 2017. At the end of its mission it was sent diving into Saturn's atmosphere on purpose.
