THE SUN

The star at the center of it all.

The Sun is a huge, glowing ball of hot gas, mostly hydrogen and helium. Deep in its core, hydrogen is fused into helium, and that releases the energy that lights and warms every planet.

It is a fairly ordinary, middle-aged star, but to us it is everything: without it there would be no daylight, no weather and no life on Earth.
